# The two alien families (decided 27 Oct 2026: Claude proposed, ChatGPT agreed + named them)

Both players can pick either family. Same counter triangle and the same numbers for both: only the look and feel differ, so
neither becomes the automatic pick.

| | SQUISH (goo aliens) | CRUNCH (rock aliens) |
|---|---|---|
| Feel | soft, round, bouncy, silly | hard, angular, heavy, stubborn |
| Silhouette | teardrop, one big eye, four nubby feet | wide beetle, crystal shell, four chunky legs |
| Colours | electric cyan; violet + mint highlights | bright amber-orange crystals; dark charcoal rock, yellow cracks |
| Material | glossy OPAQUE jelly, emissive accents | matte rock + shiny crystal plates |
| Fuse | bounce together → squash → inflate | crash together → shells interlock → giant stomp |
| Split | POP! little blobs spring out | CRACK! beetles tumble out |
| Fusion memory | Medium shows 4 glowing dots; Titan has a constellation of dots under its skin | Medium has 4 shell segments; Titan's huge plates visibly snap together |

## Forms (one rig per family, scaled; bigger forms add attachment pieces)
| Form | Matter | SQUISH size (studs) | CRUNCH size (studs) | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 1 (army = 24) | 2.5 tall, 2 wide | 2 tall, 3 wide | swarm: hops / scuttles, surrounds |
| Medium | 4 (army = 6) | 5 tall, 4.5 wide | 4.5 tall, 5.5 wide | springy lunges / horn charges |
| Titan | 24 (army = 1) | 15 tall, 12 wide | 13 tall, 15 wide | belly-flop / stomping crystal beast |

Counters (both families): swarm beats Titan in the open, Titan beats bases + mediums, mediums beat the swarm.

## Production rules
- Phone budget: small ≈ 1.5-3k tris (up to 48 on screen), medium ≈ 4-6k, Titan ≤ 15k. Textures ≤ 1024 (small ≤ 512).
- No real transparency (opaque jelly with emissive dots); fake the melt with squash/stretch, overlapping meshes, particles.
- Same skeleton and walk for all three forms of a family; animated in Blender (route A: FBX → Animation Editor).
- Kid-safe: cute-creepy, never gory.
- Map palette must stay neutral (bright grey regolith, cool shadows) so cyan and amber both pop; CRUNCH's charcoal
  must not sit on dark basalt: keep dark rock to the edges.
