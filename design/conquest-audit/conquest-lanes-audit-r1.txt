E. How to prevent one player holding the throne forever

Use timed control, not stat decay.

My recommended initial rules:

Minimum 60 seconds of protection after capturing the throne.

Maximum reign 10 minutes.

One throne challenge active at a time.

Challenge target duration 60–90 seconds.

Losing challenger must wait 2 minutes to try again.

Defender disconnects: 60-second reconnect grace, then throne vacates.

Reign expires: throne returns to NPC control, giving other players a fresh opportunity.

Defence rewards capped at one per unique challenger per reign to limit farming.

The throne is server-local, not a persistent worldwide crown. When a new hub server starts, an NPC controls it.

Players can permanently retain a cosmetic title for winning, but their production bonus ends when their reign ends.

I strongly prefer a throne that regularly changes hands to one controlled indefinitely by whoever has played the longest.

What the draft missed

Three things deserve explicit decisions before a larger build:

1. What happens after both outposts are conquered? Don't reset the player's permanent progress weekly. Preserve conquered forts and unlock optional repeatable harder invasions for additional rewards.

2. What happens in an almost-empty server? The NPC throne must remain conquerable so solo players aren't blocked from progression.

3. How does outpost ownership persist? Each player's conquered stages and payouts must be stored in their profile. The server-local throne is separate. Winning or reconnecting must not duplicate first-clear rewards.
