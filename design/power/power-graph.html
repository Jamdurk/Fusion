Alien Fusion: Power Graph

8 aliens × 3 abilities. Checked in a battle simulator (equal 32-matter armies, thousands of fights), backed by ChatGPT deep research, audited by ChatGPT.

How to read "2.2x": the winning side is as strong as an army with 2.2x its HP. Close to 1.00x = an even fight.

The counter loop

Each alien has three abilities:
  • PASSIVE: how it attacks. It makes its counter job obvious.
  • AUTO: a skill it uses by itself.
  • ORDER: the one button per form. Every unit of that form does it together.

At most 4 buttons on a phone. Cooldowns belong to the player + form, so splitting and fusing never reset them.
