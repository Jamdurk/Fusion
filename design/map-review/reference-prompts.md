# Hub reference pack: the ChatGPT image prompts (30 Oct 2026)

One NEW ChatGPT chat ("Alien Fusion Hub References"), Thinking model, images in this order; every prompt after A starts
"the SAME world as image A". Attach with prompt A: the current map sheet + the Squish and Crunch alien sheets + the Titan
hero (so the world fits the creatures). Saved results: `design\concepts\hub\`.

## STYLE (pasted into every prompt)
Stylised, polished 3D game art in the style of a top-quality 2025-2026 Roblox simulator / tycoon: chunky readable shapes,
soft PBR materials with gentle bevels, clean silhouettes, saturated but never neon-everywhere colour, readable at phone
size. Setting: a dead grey MOON being taken over by two alien species, SQUISH (cyan glowing goo with violet undersides
and bright cyan cracks) and CRUNCH (amber / orange crystal rock with glowing magma seams). Palette: charcoal, moon
silver-grey, cyan, amber, violet accents. Sky: black starfield with the big blue Earth low on the horizon. Light: a warm
white sun from one side, soft blue-violet ambient fill so shadows stay readable, small local glows on alien things.
Moon ground: pale grey regolith with small craters. No text, no labels, no logos, no UI, no people or avatars, no real
flags or brands. Kid-friendly (no gore, nothing scary).

## A. MASTER MAP (the whole hub, bird's-eye)
STYLE. ONE image: a high 3/4 bird's-eye view of the whole hub of an 8-player alien tycoon game on the moon, about 1,000 by
670 studs, ringed by tall dark basalt crater cliffs. In the CENTRE: a large impact crater (about 85 studs across) that is
the main landmark: a huge crashed alien object (a half-buried glowing alien ship or seed-pod) inside it, a broken reactor
ring hanging in the air above it held by beams of energy, a slowly turning energy structure, faint meteorite trails
streaking toward it. Around the crater: EIGHT flat square base plots (about 85 by 95 each) in two staggered groups of four,
each plot a small alien research base at a different stage (some bare with one drill, some fully built with machines,
an incubator and a trophy pedestal), connected to the crater by wide lit paths. In the OUTER band, four themed areas,
one per side: NORTH a lunar forest (tall pale silver-white trees with twisted trunks and soft violet glowing seed-pods,
blue-grey moss, floating spores); WEST a cyan GOO MARSH (glowing cyan goo pools, mushroom caps, soft blob growths); EAST
an amber CRYSTAL CANYON (jagged orange crystals, cracked dark basalt, glowing seams); SOUTH old LANDER RUINS (an abandoned
moon outpost: lander legs, solar panels, a satellite dish, a moon rover, small meteor craters). Clear readable zones,
lots of things to walk to, not cluttered.

## B. THE CRATER LANDMARK (eye level)
