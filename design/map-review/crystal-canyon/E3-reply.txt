For non-emissive basalt and sand, the glow column indicates warm colour accents, not actual emitted light.

Emissive mask recommendations

Crystals: Keep most facets non-emissive. Concentrate emission toward the inner core and fractures, with brighter edges baked into the ColorMap.

Magma: Use an approximately white-yellow core, orange middle and deep-red boundary. Limit strong emission to narrow crack interiors.

Basalt: Metalness 0 throughout. Preserve visible grey surface detail rather than making the rock near-black.

Ground: Bake scattered amber grit into the albedo; avoid emissive terrain except for designated magma cracks.

Roblox EmissiveStrength is a multiplier, not an absolute brightness measurement. Excessive strength can clamp highlights toward white, so test each mask under actual Studio lighting.
