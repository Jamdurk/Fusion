2. The recommended tycoon structure

The crucial design decision is two linked progression tracks:

Army track: Incubator → more aliens → Launch Rig upgrades → Forts → Throne.

Factory track: Drill → Refinery → Pickaxes → Dense Rocks → Extractors.

Players can alternate between them. Don't force someone who wants to fight to purchase every factory machine first.

A key change from the earlier draft: the first drill must be cheap, and the Incubator must arrive before expensive upgrades. An 80–100 Rock first drill would already cost more than a minute of sustained T1 mining, before accounting for the first battle.

These are proposed prices, not Roblox industry averages. They're designed around Alien Fusion's measured mining rates.

Purchase gates

The first Incubator and basic pickaxes require only Rock and their previous machine/tool prerequisites. They should never require a fort victory.

Launch Rig I becomes recommended when the player owns roughly 10 matter; Rig II when they own 14; Rig III after Fort 2 and approximately 20 owned matter; Rig IV after Fort 2 and approximately 24 owned matter. These are visibility/recommendation conditions, not necessarily purchase restrictions on matter ownership.

Fort 1 strengthens the Goo Extractor. Fort 2 strengthens the Crystal Extractor and opens the advanced launch progression. Neither fort blocks players from mining its family's DNA.

Important: The times in the table are not cumulative promises. A player who prioritizes the fastest 16-alien army may delay the Refinery, extractors and tool upgrades. A player who enjoys building will buy more machinery and reach the full army later.

Two changes I strongly recommend

First: give every new player 12 Goo DNA once. This is exactly one starter Squish Egg. It guarantees that even someone who loses Fort 1 can experience hatching by minute 3.

Fort 1's proposed first-clear reward becomes 60 Moon Rock + 24 Goo DNA + 12 Crystal DNA, so it also introduces Crunch without forcing a long trip to Crystal Canyon.

Second: let the Incubator keep hatching while the player is mining. After choosing an egg, players can enable repeat hatching, spending 12 matching DNA per egg until they run out or stop it. The basic chamber handles one egg at a time, taking 12 seconds each. Completed aliens enter the collection automatically.

Without this, repeatedly walking 200 studs back from Goo Marsh to hatch individual eggs could break the 5–8-minute target.

No paid auto-hatch advantage, no extra currency and no change to cosmetic rarity rules.

8. What replaces rebirth?

Permanent collection, skill and customization.

Once a player owns 32 of each species, the next hatch is about appearances rather than army strength. Keep the existing rule: the player chooses whether to apply a newly rolled look to an owned alien, with favourite locks and no automatic replacement.

Moon Rock continues funding cosmetic base expansions, decorations and optional production upgrades. DNA continues funding cosmetic hatching. Forts offer changing tactical challenges; the throne offers social prestige.

The player should eventually be able to build a base that looks noticeably different from another player's, without owning a stronger PvP army.

I would not add a second floor, rebirth multiplier or another currency simply to manufacture endless numerical progression. A later second base row could be a cosmetic extension, but isn't needed for launch.

This also follows Roblox's core-loop principle: repeated actions need a meaningful progression engine, not just repetition for its own sake.

10. Final decisions for Claude and James

GO for the one-plot tycoon slice. GO WITH TESTING for the complete economy.

The three biggest choices in this spec are:

Starter 12 Goo DNA: My pick is yes, to guarantee an early hatch.

Repeat hatching: My pick is yes, as a free convenience feature. Otherwise the mining-to-hatching loop involves too much walking.

Factory versus army progression: My pick is two parallel branches, with the army branch always taking priority in the next-goal UI.

The key numerical risk is not the first drill price. It's whether a player can afford the later Launch Rigs while also spending enough time gathering DNA to reach 32 aliens.
