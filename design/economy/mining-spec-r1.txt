Meteorites

Meteorites are the big shared mining event.

Frequency: Every 105 seconds, plus or minus 15 seconds.

Location: One of eight predetermined crater-impact sockets, clear of lanes and the throne.

Warning: 10-second sky streak and landing marker.

HP: 60 + 20 for each additional player within 200 studs when it lands, capped at 180.

Lifetime: 75 seconds after impact, then disappears.

Rewards: Each eligible participant receives 30 Moon Rock, 12 Goo DNA and 12 Crystal DNA.

Every participating player receives the full listed reward; it is not divided among players. To qualify, a player must contribute at least five damage across at least two valid swings.

A player can break a meteorite alone. Multiple players simply break it faster and share the spectacle.

Only one meteorite can be active at a time.

C. Deposit spawning
Your 85 × 95-stud plot

Start with three small rocks, one big rock and one starter Goo pocket.

Use six fixed spawn sockets in a clearly marked mining corner or strip of the plot, away from construction pads, conveyors, the Launch Pad and player spawn.

The rocks respawn individually after depletion, at their own socket or another free socket. Never spawn them on top of a machine or player.

Later Rock-mining upgrades can increase available sockets and improve refresh rates, giving better pickaxes enough deposits to work on.

Initial cap: Four active Rock deposits plus one Goo pocket per plot. Upgraded cap: six Rock deposits plus the Goo pocket.

Crucial multiplayer rule: Ordinary biome deposits are per-player harvestable, even though everyone sees the same landmark.

When one player depletes a vein, it goes dark or transparent for that player only. Another player can still mine it. The server tracks each player's depletion cooldown.

Meteorites are the exception: they're genuinely shared, with server-wide HP and individual contribution tracking.

E. Anti-abuse and mobile performance
Server authority

The client sends only StartMining(nodeId) and StopMining requests.

The server owns the mining timer, deposit HP, player-specific depletion state and reward calculation.

For every swing, the server verifies that the player is alive, the tool is owned and equipped, the deposit is active, the player is within 12 studs, and the target is reachable without an obstructing wall.

The server enforces the 0.7-second swing interval. Extra clicks, auto-clickers and remote spam cannot produce additional hits.

On break, mark the deposit depleted and award resources once in the same authoritative operation. Never let two simultaneous hit requests pay the same node twice.

Use a short request-rate limiter in addition to swing timing. This aligns with Roblox's current client-server security guidance.
