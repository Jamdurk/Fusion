## 0. The game in one paragraph
You crash-land on a moon with one alien. You mine meteorites to find new alien species and rare mutations, feed and level
your aliens, and play quick minigames for upgrade materials. Then you take your army of 32 into short battles (bot nests,
raids, 1v1 players) where you never pick attacks: the aliens fight by themselves and YOU decide where they go, when they
fuse into bigger forms or split back into swarms, and when the big Orders fire. Battles pay out the materials and keys
that open better meteorites. Collect everything, build the coolest army, prove you're the best commander.

## 1. The loop **[JAMES: which side is the main one]**
```
 MINE meteorites ──> FIND species + mutations ──> LEVEL + FEED aliens ──> BATTLE (fuse / split) ──> rewards + keys
        ^                                                                                          │
        └──────────────────────── better meteorites, new moons ───────────────────────────────────┘
```
My pick: **both halves every session, battles short and frequent.** A 10-minute session = ~5 min hub (mine, open, level,
a minigame) + 2 battles of 3 min. Reason: the collecting half is the Roblox hook (simulator / brain-rot players come
back for the next find); the battle half is what NO other game has (fuse / split) and stops it being one more simulator.
Neither half may need the other to be fun for 60 s: a new player battles in their first minute (tutorial) and finds
their first rare in their first five.

## 2. Getting aliens (the collecting half)
- **One alien = one SPECIES, not one unit.** You never grind 32 copies: owning the Drip species means your army can field
  32 matter of Drips (and fuse them into Twins, Brutes, a Titan). Start: SQUISH (Drip line). First find: CRUNCH.
- **Meteorites** fall in the Crash Site every 60-90 s (a server event everyone sees and runs to: social + urgency).
  Tap-mine with the pickaxe (each strike a timed tap: perfect = x2 damage); bigger meteorites need more players or a
  better pickaxe. Cracked open: matter shards (always), stardust (always), a SPECIES CORE (rare), a MUTATION (rarer).
- **Mutations** = the brain-rot collecting hook. Rarity tiers with real odds shown on the meteorite (felt rarity rule:
  rare = <= 0.5 % and announced): Normal / Glowing (8 %) / Crystal (2 %) / Shadow (0.5 %) / Rainbow (0.1 %) / Cosmic
  (0.02 %, server-wide banner). A mutation changes the whole species' look (skin, glow, fusion burst, Titan aura) and
  carries a tiny +2 % stat badge (flex, not power). Equip one per species.
- **Keys** from battles open SEALED meteorites (better odds) and later the next moon (new meteorite pool, new species).
- **Index** (the collection book): every species x mutation; filling a row gives a title / emote / pickaxe skin.

## 3. Levelling (the grind) + the cap
- Each FORM of each species levels separately (Drip, Twin, Brute, Titan): XP from battles it took part in + ALIEN FOOD
  (crafted from matter shards; the minigames give bonus food). Level 1-30.
- **The cap (James: "cap the abilities at a certain threshold"):** a maxed form has at most **+10 % HP and +10 %
  damage** (1.21x combined strength). Why that number: the power graph's weakest counter edge is 1.98x
  (`09-POWER-GRAPH.md` §6), so a maxed army can still lose to a correct fuse / split counter. Grind matters; skill wins.
- Levels also unlock things that are NOT stats: Order variants at 10 (same power, different look/shape, e.g. Goo
  Tsunami as a ring instead of a lane... audit each), the form's mutation slot at 5, a victory pose at 20, gold name at 30.
- **Rebirth:** none at launch (research: rebirth treadmills = top genre complaint).

## 4. Minigames (points for upgrades) — 2 at launch
| Minigame | Where | Gives | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meteor mining** (the core one) | Crash Site, every 60-90 s | shards, stardust, cores, mutations | 20-40 s |
| **UFO abduction run** | the sky above the Crash Site | alien food (beam up moon critters before the timer; streak multiplier) | 60 s |
Later: alien racing (your Drip on an obstacle course), crater defence (wave PvE). Each must feed the same currencies.

## 5. Battles (the fusion half)
### 5.1 Modes
| Mode | Who | Levels count? | Rewards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nest raids** (PvE) | you vs bot armies on harder moons, a boss Titan every 5th | yes | keys, food, XP |
| **Casual 1v1** | players, matched by ARMY POWER (sum of form levels) +-10 % | yes (capped +21 %) | keys, XP, stardust |
| **Ranked 1v1** | players, matched by rating | **no: everyone at level 1 stats** (pure fuse / split skill) | rank, season rewards |
| **Friend battle** | private | toggle | none |
### 5.2 The player in battle: a UFO **[JAMES]**
In the hub you walk with a pickaxe (normal Roblox avatar). In a battle you beam up into your UFO above the field
(camera tilted 55°, follows the UFO, pinch to zoom). The UFO can't be attacked (no avatar combat to balance), its beam
is the fuse control, and it fits the theme. Alternatives: walk with your army (Command An Army style; the avatar
becomes a second game to balance), or a plain RTS camera (reads "not Roblox").
### 5.3 Controls (PC + phone, five verbs; everything else is the AI)
| Verb | Phone | PC | Server |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOVE | tap the ground | right click | selected forms attack-move there |
| FOCUS | tap an enemy | right click it | selected forms prefer it (AI score x3, prey rules still apply) |
| SELECT | 4 form chips, bottom left (none lit = all) | 1-4, ` | which forms MOVE / FOCUS apply to |
| FUSE / SPLIT | hold the beam over your units / chip +/- | F / G | the nearest eligible group fuses (rules §2: distances, times, 5 s lock, HP % carries) |
| ORDER | the form's big button, bottom right (max 4) | Q W E R | power graph Orders; glow = good moment |
HOLD = double-tap the rally ring. RETREAT = all home at 1.3x speed, can't attack, 8 s cooldown. No drags; buttons >= 64 px.
Fusing / splitting units take x1.25 damage, can't attack, show a 0.3 s tell (punishable). Max 2 transformations at once.
### 5.4 A 1v1, start to finish (210 s, cap 240)
| Time | Phase | |
|---|---|---|
| -20 s | DEPLOY | pick race + STARTING MIX freely (any legal split of 32; presets SWARM / BALANCED / TITAN); hidden from the enemy until 0:00 |
| 0:00 | GATES OPEN | armies drop through the forward gates; first contact ~10 s |
| 2:30 | OVERCHARGE | Order cooldowns x0.75, core shields off for good, music + sky change |
| 3:30 | TIME | healthier core % wins; within 1 % = 30 s sudden death; then draw |
Defeated matter returns after 12 s at the gate as F1 (rules ★).
### 5.5 The arena: each form has a job
| Thing | Rule | Best at it |
|---|---|---|
| Matter Core (each base) | 12,000 HP; 50 % shield while its owner holds the crater or has a unit within 40; shields off at 2:30 | Titans (x2.5 vs bases) |
| Fusion Reactor (crater) | capture speed = YOUR units in the ring minus the enemy's (units, not matter); holding it: your Orders x0.85 cooldown, enemy core loses the crater shield | Swarms |
| Fusion vents (N + S routes) | fuse here in half the time with a 2.5 s lock; 15 s recharge | fusing on the move |
| Defence pylons (2 per base) | 1,500 HP, 40 dps, x2 vs swarm: a lone swarm rush isn't a win | anything, first |
**The map [JAMES]:** a 1v1 doesn't need the full 1,008 x 672 moon. My pick: cut the battle arena to roughly the original
360 x 240 design (`02-MAP.md`: bases, crater, canyon, boulder plain = about 30 s base to base for a swarm), keep the big
moon's look and props, and use the rest of the terrain as the Crash Site hub. Smaller = more fights per minute, fewer
streamed parts on phones. Test both sizes bot-vs-bot (match length, fights per minute).

## 6. What brings players back (end goals)
Three kinds of player, one world: **the collector** (fill the Index, chase a Cosmic mutation), **the grinder** (max every
form, clear every nest moon), **the competitor** (ranked season, leaderboards). Daily tasks (3 a day, plain words),
weekly meteor shower event (better odds for an hour), seasons every 6 weeks (new mutation set + ranked reset), new moons
= new species (Mars armour-break, Europa freeze: sidegrades with new verbs).

## 7. Money (Robux) **[JAMES]**
Fine: cosmetic mutations' look-alikes NOT sold (finds stay earned), UFO hulls + beams, pickaxe skins, emotes, battle pass,
extra equip slots, private servers, x2 mining-luck passes (luck, never stats). NEVER: matter, HP / damage above the cap,
levels for Robux in ranked (ranked is level-free anyway), pay-to-skip Index rows.

## 8. Roblox build (the depth part)
### 8.1 Places + matchmaking
- **One experience, two places:** HUB (Crash Site: mining, minigames, social, queue; 20-30 players) and BATTLE (one match
  per reserved server). Nest raids can run in the same battle place with a bot side.
- **Queue:** MemoryStoreService sorted map per mode + band (army power for casual, rating for ranked); entries expire;
  one hub server claims pairs with UpdateAsync (no double-claim); band widens every 10 s; 20 s alone = bot match.
  TeleportService:ReserveServer + TeleportAsync (TeleportData = mode + bot difficulty only; the battle server re-reads
  the real profile, never trusts TeleportData for levels or ownership).
- **Reconnect:** 45 s to rejoin the same reserved server (access code in MemoryStore); the bot plays the army meanwhile.
### 8.2 Authority + network
- Client sends INTENTS only (Move, Focus, Select, Fuse, Split, Order, Retreat; Mine strike; Open meteorite); rate-limited,
  validated (owner, alive, cooldown, distance, matter sums, phase). Never positions, damage, rolls or HP from the client.
- Battle server: THINK 5 Hz staggered, MOVE 20 Hz, combat authoritative; positions 10 Hz over UnreliableRemoteEvent
  (64 units ~640 B, < 1,000 B limit); spawns, deaths, fusions, HP, Orders, tells reliable. Units = server state + light
  hit models (no Humanoids, Anchored, CanCollide off); clients animate their own copies (AlienAnimator, built).
- **Drops are server rolls** (meteorite contents rolled when the server cracks it, from server-side tables; the odds shown
  in the UI come from the same table). Mining damage: server checks strike rate + distance (no auto-clicker gains past
  the cap).
### 8.3 Data
- One session-locked profile per player (ProfileStore pattern over DataStoreService): species, mutations, form levels +
  XP, currencies, keys, Index, rating, daily tasks, settings. Save on battle end, on leave, every 3 min.
- Rare finds (Shadow and above) logged to an append-only history (support + anti-dupe checks).
- Ranked rating: Glicko-2-lite; leaderboards OrderedDataStore per season; purchases via an idempotent ProcessReceipt.
### 8.4 Phone + performance
- Measured: 96 AI units 60 fps on a base iPhone 17 (James, 29 Oct). A battle = 64 units: inside budget.
- Still owed: a budget phone at 64 units; the BATTLE SERVER's frame time with 64 units fighting (MicroProfiler; target
  heartbeat >= 55 Hz, else THINK 4 Hz first); the HUB with 30 players + falling meteorites + effects.
### 8.5 Anti-exploit
Intents only; server rolls; rate limits; matter conservation asserted every fuse / split; results written by the battle
server only; the UFO is checked for speed + a leash.
