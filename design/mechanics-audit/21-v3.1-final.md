## 0. The game in one paragraph
You crash-land on a moon with one alien species. Meteorites fall; you mine them to find a new species and rare-looking
mutations. You take your 32 matter of aliens into short battles where you never pick attacks: the aliens fight by
themselves and YOU decide where they go, when they FUSE into bigger forms or SPLIT back into swarms, and when the big
Orders fire. The thing no other game has: turning a losing fight around by reshaping your army mid-battle.

## 1. The loop (no fixed time split)
MINE a meteorite -> FIND (species, mutation, materials) -> BATTLE -> keys + materials -> REMATCH or MINE again.
First-session targets (measured in the slice):
| By | The player has |
|---|---|
| 60 s | started their first battle (tutorial, vs an Easy bot) |
| 3 min | seen a spectacular first fusion (4 Brutes -> Titan, scripted moment in the tutorial) AND made their first find |
| 10 min | done one full mine -> find -> battle -> mine cycle, and been offered ONE clear next goal |
After every reward the screen shows ONE next goal ("Open a Sealed meteorite: 1 more key"), never five currencies.

## 2. Getting aliens
- **One find = one SPECIES** (never 32 copies): owning it lets your army field 32 matter of it.
- Launch: 2 species (SQUISH, CRUNCH). New combat species later (each needs a kit, AI profile and balance pass).
- **Meteorites** fall in the Crash Site every 60-90 s (everyone sees it and runs to it). Tap-mine with a pickaxe
  (timed strike: perfect = x2). Contents rolled by the server: materials (always), stardust (always), sometimes a
  mutation, rarely a species core (only until you own both).
- **Guaranteed early finds:** the first Sealed meteorite (from the tutorial battle's key) always contains CRUNCH; the
  third meteorite you mine always contains a Glowing mutation. Rarity is the long goal, never the first goal.
  A guaranteed find REPLACES that meteorite's mutation roll (never added on top, never overwritten by a rarer roll);
  the profile stores which guarantees are used, so a retry or rejoin can't pay one twice.
- **Mutations are COSMETIC ONLY** (no stats anywhere): skin, glow, fusion burst, Titan aura. Odds shown on the meteorite
  (felt-rarity rule): Glowing 8 % / Crystal 2 % / Shadow 0.5 % / Rainbow 0.1 % / Cosmic 0.02 % (server banner).
- **Show it off:** your equipped mutation follows you in the Crash Site as a pet-sized alien, shows on your units in
  battle, and on the pre-battle card the opponent sees.
- **Index** (collection book): species x mutation; filling a row gives a title / emote.

## 3. Levelling: one mastery level per species
- **Species mastery 1-20** from battles + alien food (made from materials). One bar per species, not per form.
- Unlocks along the bar: mutation slot (2), a victory pose (5), form portraits / name colours (10, 15), gold title (20).
- **Combat bonus PvE ONLY** (nest raids): up to +10 % HP and +10 % damage at mastery 20 (James's "cap at a threshold").
- **PvP is normalised** (casual AND ranked): everyone fights at the same stats; skill decides. Revisit capped bonuses in
  casual only after a test compares level 20 vs level 1 armies over every counter edge **[TEST]**.
- Order variants: COSMETIC only, same hit shape and timing (a lane stays a lane).
- No rebirth at launch.

## 4. Minigames: mining only at launch
Meteor mining is the minigame. The UFO abduction run comes later, only if the loop works, and never as a daily chore.

## 5. Battles
### 5.1 Modes
| Mode | When | Stats |
|---|---|---|
| Tutorial + **nest raids** (PvE vs bots, harder moons, a boss Titan every 5th) | slice | mastery bonus applies |
| **Casual 1v1** | after the slice | normalised |
| **Ranked 1v1** | later | normalised |
After every battle: **REMATCH** (same opponent / bot, one tap) or BACK TO MINING. No long trip back first.
### 5.2 You are a UFO **[JAMES]**
Hub: your Roblox avatar with a pickaxe. Battle: you beam up into your UFO above the field (camera tilted 55°, follows
the UFO, pinch zoom). Can't be attacked, its beam is the fuse control, fits the theme. To test on a real phone:
can you see your units, tell yours from theirs, and hit the right group with a thumb.
### 5.3 Controls: picking WHICH units (the r1 fix)
| Verb | Phone | PC | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SELECT a group** | tap one of your units: it and its kind within 12 studs light up (a "group") | left click | the next MOVE / FOCUS applies to that group only |
| SELECT a form | form chips, bottom left (count on each) | 1-4 | all units of that form |
| SELECT all | double-tap empty ground / ALL chip | ` | everything |
| **MOVE** | tap the ground | right click | the selection attack-moves there |
| **FOCUS** | tap an enemy | right click it | the selection prefers it (AI score x3, prey rules still apply) |
| **FUSE** | hold the BEAM button, drag the beam ring onto your units; the ring shows what it will make (ghost silhouette + "2 Drips -> Twin" / "4 Brutes -> TITAN"); let go = do it, drag off the field = cancel | hold F at the mouse, release | the server fuses the eligible group INSIDE the ring (locked rule distances + times) |
| **SPLIT** | tap the beam button once to switch it to SPLIT (red ring), drop it on a unit | G at the mouse | that unit splits (locked times) |
| **ORDER** | the form's big button, bottom right (max 4) | Q W E R | power graph Orders; glow = good moment |
- **Two ways, one server action** (r2): tap a unit to light its group, then tap **FUSE** (the fast way, no drag); or drag
  the beam for deliberate targeting (the signature move). Both send the SAME request: the exact unit ids in the preview.
- **The ring sits 60 px above the finger** (pulled inward near screen edges) so the finger never hides the preview.
- **Honest preview:** the client highlights the exact units it will send; the server checks THOSE ids with the locked
  rules (2 F1 within 6, 2 F2 within 9, four F3 within 18 studs of THEIR centroid) at the moment of release. If they
  drifted out of range, it cancels with a clear "too far apart" pop, and never fuses a different group.
- The ring's look follows the rule (for 4 Brutes it draws their centroid + the 18-stud circle around it).
- **Tutorial teaches three verbs only:** MOVE, FUSE, SPLIT. Orders come in the 2nd battle; FOCUS, HOLD, RETREAT exist but
  are never required to win a nest raid.
- **[TEST]** on a real phone before 5.3 locks: can a 10-12 year old pick the right group, fuse, split, in under 3 s each?
- **Acceptance test (r3):** move the selected units, start another transformation on one of them, and release the beam
  at the same moment: the server rejects stale / ineligible ids without spending matter, starting a lock or cooldown,
  or transforming any other unit.
- Fusing / splitting units can't attack, take x1.25 damage, show a 0.3 s tell. Max 2 transformations at once.
- HOLD = double-tap the rally ring. RETREAT = all home at 1.3x speed, can't attack, 8 s cooldown.
- Every button >= 64 px; no drags except the beam (one deliberate drag, the game's signature move).
### 5.4 A 1v1, start to finish (210 s, cap 240)
| Time | Phase | |
|---|---|---|
| -20 s | DEPLOY | pick race + starting mix: ANY legal split of 32 matter (presets SWARM / BALANCED / TITAN are shortcuts only); hidden until 0:00 |
| 0:00 | GATES OPEN | armies drop through their forward gates |
| 2:30 | OVERCHARGE | Order cooldowns x0.75, core shields off for good, music + sky change |
| 3:30 | TIME | healthier core % wins; within 1 % = 30 s sudden death; then draw |
### 5.5 Arena rules (ALL [TEST] in the sim before they lock)
| Thing | Rule (one each, no overlaps) |
|---|---|
| Matter Core | 12,000 HP. **Shield rule: takes 50 % damage while its owner holds the Fusion Reactor; never after 2:30.** |
| Fusion Reactor (crater) | capture speed = your units in the ring minus the enemy's (units, not matter); held: your Order cooldowns x0.85 |
| Fusion vents | units on a vent fuse in HALF the animation time; the 5 s lock stays (locked rule) |
| Defence pylons (2 per base) | 1,500 HP, 40 dps, x2 vs swarm |
| Respawn | defeated matter returns after 12 s as F1 at the gate |
Sim questions before locking (the power sim gets bases, a reactor and respawns): does the swarm's capture edge + the
reactor's cooldown cut snowball? Is a Titan killed (16 matter back in 12 s, as Drips) better or worse for its owner
than 16 Drips dying one by one? Average match length 160-240 s? Comebacks: the side behind at 1:30 wins >= 20 %?

## 8. Roblox build
### 8.1 The slice: ONE place
Hub + arena in one place, bot battles only, no saving. Proves the loop before any cross-server work.
### 8.2 After the slice: two places (hub + battle), done right
- **Match making (r2 fixes T1-T3):**
  - Queues: `MemoryStoreService:GetQueue("q_<mode>_<band>", 30)` (the 2nd argument is the 30 s invisibility timeout)
    per mode AND band (casual: by mastery-free MMR band; ranked: rating band of 100). After 15 s waiting a player is
    ALSO put in the neighbouring band; after 30 s, a bot match.
  - Pairing: `local items, readId = queue:ReadAsync(2, true, 5)` (count, allOrNothing = true: a full pair or nothing,
    wait 5 s). Those entries are hidden from every other server until the timeout.
  - **Player lease** (one entry, one match per player): a sorted map `player:<userId>` holds {state, matchId, until}.
    The pairing server sets BOTH leases with `UpdateAsync` only if each is "queued" (not "matched" or expired); if
    either fails, it releases the one it took and lets the queue items time out back (no RemoveAsync).
  - Only when both leases are held: write `match:<id>` (only if absent), `RemoveAsync(readId)`, then teleport.
    Entries older than 60 s or whose player left the hub are rejected at the lease step.
  - Rewards are granted once per (match id, player): the profile keeps the last 20 match ids it has been paid for.
- **Reserved servers:** `TeleportOptions.ReservedServerId = HttpService:GenerateGUID(false)` (verified in the live docs
  30 Oct: ReserveServer access codes and ShouldReserveServer are deprecated); the same id routes a reconnect.
- **One absent-player rule (T4):** from the start of the match, a player who isn't there (never arrived, or dropped) has
  his army played by the Normal bot. He has **45 s** from the moment he went missing to (re)join the same reserved
  server and take control back mid-match (no restart). After 45 s the bot keeps playing to the end, the match counts as
  his loss and gives no rating change to the opponent if the bot then wins (no farming leavers).
- **Teleport failures:** handle TeleportInitFailed (3 retries, backoff). If the teleport finally fails after the hub
  let go of the profile, the hub re-takes the lock and puts the player back in the queue screen; nothing is lost.
- **Profiles between places:** session-locked profiles (ProfileStore pattern). The battle server waits for the lock with
  the store's built-in retry (the hub may still be releasing it: the documented fast-transition race) before admitting
  the player; rewards are written with the match id so a retry can't double them.
- **Admission (T5):** the battle server never trusts TeleportData: on PlayerAdded it reads `match:<id>` from MemoryStore
  and checks UserId, team and that the match is live; anyone else is kicked to the hub.
- **Still required before multiplayer is built (ChatGPT r3, NO-GO until done; they don't hold up the bot slice):**
  - M1 one queue-session token per player (entering the neighbouring band re-uses it); pairing checks the token, so a
    stale entry in another band is rejected after a match; a rollback releases only leases this exact attempt owns
    (conditional UpdateAsync on the attempt id), never one another server took.
  - M2 the 30 s bot fallback flips the player's lease to "bot" atomically (no PvP pairing after it); leases and match
    records carry an expiry covering the match + reconnect window, so a crashed pairing server recovers by itself.
  - M3 rewards = one settlement step: the reward and the processed match id are written to the profile in the SAME
    profile update (permanent, not a last-20 list), so a retry finds it already settled.
  - M4 leavers: after the 45 s grace the absent player FORFEITS: the opponent gets the win whatever the stand-in bot does
    afterwards; no rating is earned for the rest of that fight; leaver penalties are a separate rule.
### 8.3 Authority + network
- Client sends INTENTS only (select / move / focus / beam fuse / split / order / retreat; mine strike; open meteorite):
  rate-limited, validated (owner, alive, cooldown, distance, matter sums, phase, target exists and is legal), with a
  per-player sequence number (no replays).
- Server: THINK 5 Hz staggered, MOVE 20 Hz, combat authoritative. Positions 10 Hz on UnreliableRemoteEvent packed in a
  `buffer`, < 1,000 bytes (chunk if over), sequence numbers: a client drops any snapshot older than the newest it has
  applied (and any for a unit generation that no longer exists); a reliable full-state correction every 2 s carries
  its own server time and only overwrites state older than it (reliable and unreliable channels don't share ordering).
  **Measure the real packet size** (the 640 B figure is an estimate). 5 / 20 / 10 Hz are starting values, not budgets.
- Drops rolled on the server from server tables (the shown odds come from the same table).
### 8.4 Performance: what's proven and what isn't
- Proven: CLIENT rendering, 96 AI units at 60 fps on a base iPhone 17.
- NOT proven (the next measurements): a LIVE two-client match on Roblox servers: server frame time (target < 16.7 ms
  steady), AI CPU per tick, network KB/s per player, 30-min session memory; a budget phone at 64 units incl. thermal
  throttling; the hub with 30 players + meteorites.
