## Gotchas found (also in LESSONS.md)
- Smooth terrain never renders perfectly flat: even exact -2.00 voxels showed 0.8-2.0 (a half-full top voxel bumps
  ~1.6, a full one ~0.9). For spawn floors / shelves lay a thin flat slab over the levelled pad.
- Measure clearance with EVERY obstacle (pylons, lip rocks) and every 1 deg: the wreck-only check said 15.9, the real
  pinch was 9 at the south pylon (the wreck sits off-centre). Rays from above per stud along each radius.
- Regenerating a biome at shifted coordinates is NOT the same ground moved: every mask it blends with (plain, highland
  noise) must move too, or the edges change (an 8-stud ridge appeared at the canyon mouth). Sampling the finished old
  heightfield through a displacement field moves everything at once, blend bands included.
- A stretch shears the passes between biomes: re-cut them between their moved ends.
- The marsh / canyon paint passes re-wrote their whole box, so "hand edits" = the whole box; a crater box from the old
  arena paste holds old ground out to +-160: only treat r < 118 there as hand work.
- screen_capture with camera_position jumps the camera instantly: freshly rewritten terrain chunks show as black holes.
  Park workspace.CurrentCamera at the spot, wait ~4 s, then capture with no position.
